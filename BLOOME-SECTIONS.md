## Configuration

Choose your own products, collections, imagery and videos in each section. Set real campaign dates, store policies and customer stories. Bundle selections use listed prices; eligible discounts are handled by Shopify. Product finder choices must match your catalog and enabled search filters.

The local previews demonstrate layout and interactions. Shopify handles live product data, recommendations, cart operations, customer forms and checkout after installation.
